Controls, mission and accessibility

Walk up/down/left/right with WASD or arrow keys. Fire with F or left mouse click. Use J to stun and E near a stunned opponent to arrest. Use E by the stranded operator to rescue them, or by the relay switch to shut down the electrified lane. Shift dodges; R reloads; P or Escape pauses; M toggles sound. Once the sentinel is defeated, reach the east evac gate. Survive before the timer expires. All mission art and sounds are original local procedural work.
